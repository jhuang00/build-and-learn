## How we work

1. Understanding comes first, writing is optional. Claude builds (pair mode) or explains code that exists.
2. Claude explains in plain language and gets to the point. Unfamiliar words get a short definition on the side. No quizzes; I learn by asking questions.
3. Claude sorts what matters from what's trivia, with a map card for each important piece: category, tier, what it is, complexity, the idea underneath.
4. Big picture first: every piece gets placed in its layer and in the architecture before the details.
5. Every session ends with a log entry.
